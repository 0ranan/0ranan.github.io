# win10-显示器颜色校正

> 本文转载自：[win10系统显示器颜色怎么校正-百度经验 (baidu.com)](https://jingyan.baidu.com/article/a3761b2bef739b1576f9aadf.html)

1. 首先我们点击电脑左下角的微软按钮，弹出的界面，我们点击设置；

2. ![win10系统显示器颜色怎么校正](https://exp-picture.cdn.bcebos.com/b1454a1bd10ff2265578a9bb9c99e92abbb8a47b.jpg?x-bce-process=image%2Fresize%2Cm_lfit%2Cw_500%2Climit_1%2Fformat%2Cf_jpg%2Fquality%2Cq_80)

3. 弹出的界面，我们点击系统；

4. ![win10系统显示器颜色怎么校正](https://exp-picture.cdn.bcebos.com/555acf0ff2260d9a68ec8f2c622abab84340a57b.jpg?x-bce-process=image%2Fresize%2Cm_lfit%2Cw_500%2Climit_1%2Fformat%2Cf_jpg%2Fquality%2Cq_80)

5. 弹出的界面，我们点击高级显示设置；

6. ![win10系统显示器颜色怎么校正](https://exp-picture.cdn.bcebos.com/d04eec260d9a310e4d7b719f31b842406bfea27b.jpg?x-bce-process=image%2Fresize%2Cm_lfit%2Cw_500%2Climit_1%2Fformat%2Cf_jpg%2Fquality%2Cq_80)

8. ![win10系统显示器颜色怎么校正](https://exp-picture.cdn.bcebos.com/f367139a310e1799b2c8220dc9406afec214a37b.jpg?x-bce-process=image%2Fresize%2Cm_lfit%2Cw_500%2Climit_1%2Fformat%2Cf_jpg%2Fquality%2Cq_80)

9. 弹出的界面，我们点击颜色管理，然后再点击颜色管理；

10. ![win10系统显示器颜色怎么校正](https://exp-picture.cdn.bcebos.com/0cdb2f0e1799e92ae65adaf5e1fec314f0c5a07b.jpg?x-bce-process=image%2Fresize%2Cm_lfit%2Cw_500%2Climit_1%2Fformat%2Cf_jpg%2Fquality%2Cq_80)

12. ![win10系统显示器颜色怎么校正](https://exp-picture.cdn.bcebos.com/304f0999e92abab81fa2f24b4814f1c594eea17b.jpg?x-bce-process=image%2Fresize%2Cm_lfit%2Cw_500%2Climit_1%2Fformat%2Cf_jpg%2Fquality%2Cq_80)
